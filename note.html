Question
What is Anki's spaced repetition algorithm?
Answer
Spaced repetition algorithms dynamically adjust review intervals based on memory strength. Using algorithms like SuperMemo's SM-2, they schedule reviews at the critical point of forgetting to efficiently consolidate long-term memory.
